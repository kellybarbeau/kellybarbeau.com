About

I've spent the last decade building financial products, mostly in lending. The work I like best is taking something slow and manual and figuring out how AI or automation can make it feel effortless. I came up as a software engineer, so I'm happiest when I'm close to the technical details.

At Kiavi, I lead a team of PMs and own the roadmap for rebuilding our origination system as an AI-first underwriting platform. I also spend a lot of time helping other teams figure out where AI actually fits into their workflows.

Off the clock, I'm a mom, a wife, and a chronic over-planner of vacations. Winters are for chairlifts, the rest of the year is mostly national parks, and I write about it all here.
